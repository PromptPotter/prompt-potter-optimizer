# feed-version: a subject's version only moves forward (cloud · main)

`briefs/common.md` binds you (read BASE FIRST); the base is **`origin/main` at the sha the dispatch
names**. Push to `arc/feed-version`. **The operator's standard: "we always want to go to the full
perfect shape at the root"**: no retry, no wait, no client-side dedupe beside the served fact.

## The row (read its full text in the ledger at your base)
**The webapp's live state lands on the wrong subject or the wrong version**: feed versions are
unordered hashes (`application/cycle_reads.py::feed_versions`, a sha of `repr`), so a stale read
reverts a draft edit, an ack shows applied before the feed moves, and a second press files a second
start-run. Root: a monotone version per subject.

**Seen in the gate (2026-11-19, the orchestrator):** the full gate's playwright check, under its
load, failed `e2e/cold/pools-checkin.spec.ts:120` ("the browser switches the host and narrows the
pool…"): after the entry chip lit from the served draft, a click on "All entries" left it
`aria-pressed="true"`, and across the 15 s wait the chip was disabled 8 times (the served `active`
fell back to `["*"]`, the lone chip) and enabled 25 times (back to holding the entry): the served
draft flipped between an older and a newer version. The spec passes 10/10 alone
(`--repeat-each 5`). `NodeKnobsPanel.tsx::PoolPicker` computes the next `active` from the served
`pool.active`, so a reverted read also feeds a stale list into the next write.

## The shape
Every subject the feed names carries a version that only moves forward (a store's own counter or
the ledger offset it last folded, whichever the store already keeps; never a clock, never a hash), and
the read cache (`webapp/lib/read-cache.ts`) never replaces a newer read with an older one, by that
version alone. An ack names the version its write produced, so "applied" renders when the feed
reaches it, and a press that already filed waits on that version instead of filing again. One
version rule for every subject at every entry point that reads the feed (REST, webapp, MCP where it
reads). Decide where the counter lives by the fewest spellings and say why. Grep every reader of
`feed_versions` and every `useRead` consumer that writes from what it read.
