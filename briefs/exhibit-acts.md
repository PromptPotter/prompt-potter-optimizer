## The row (read its full text in the ledger at your base, § batch/29 · tv-2)
**A reader with no capability is offered acts, and each one 404s.** The exhibit's reader is built
with no capability (`application/exhibit.py::exhibit_stores`), yet `jobs/launcher/admission.py::
admitted` serves it `offers: "pause"`, and every other served act is drawn from facts that never read
the reader. A webapp lane tried a client gate keyed on the follow (`useWorkspace().exhibit`); review
dropped it: one candidate click, forest or node pick, TimeRay scrub or reload pins the path, the
gate turns off, and Pause / Skip / limits / lookahead and the critical line's Pause return, each a
404. Only the read knows whose stores it answered from (`exhibit.py::read_stores`).

The act surfaces the review found (by reading, confirm each by rendering): the run control dock and

(`LiveSegment.tsx`), lineage cleanup and "delete this empty fork" (`useCampaignForest.ts`,

## The shape
The dispatcher already gates every command on ONE map, `CommandKind.capability`
(`commands/dispatcher.py::_rung`, `shared/identity.py::has_capability`). A served read derives what
it offers from that same map applied to the read's own identity (`stores.identity`), so the gate
refuses and the read offers by one rule: an act a reader could not file is never offered, at every
entry point (REST, webapp, MCP, CLI read verbs, `embedded_run.py` where it reads an offer). Where an
act's offer is computed (`RunAdmission`, `command_reading.py`'s `acts`, `deletable_stubs`, a decision
gate's served acts, verify / fork / scenario / snippet facts), it reads the reader through that rule,
or one served fact carries the reader's acts and the surfaces read it: decide which by the fewest
spellings, and say why. No webapp-side derivation of who the reader is. A follower still SEES the
exhibit whole (its readouts, status, samples in flight); only its acts go.
