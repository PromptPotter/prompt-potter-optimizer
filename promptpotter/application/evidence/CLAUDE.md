# application/evidence/ — what a set of subjects jointly says

**Read-only**: the read names a leader; graduating one is a hand-edit.

- **Every subject kind (`campaign`, `course`, `candidate`) resolves to one searchpoint's rows and reduces through `cell_channels`**; `domain/cycle_paths.py::Subject` owns the grammar, and a sandbox is an ordinary address.
- **A candidate's cells are the ARCHIVE's rows at every address its ledger names**, never a round file's or ledger record's copy; `SubjectReading.status: measuring` marks every figure a prefix.
- **The head-to-head reads the BENCH under ONE grader** (`comparison_grader`); `head_to_head.py::pair_guard` labels a contrast, and a `differs` contrast sits outside the Holm/BH family ([decisions](../../../docs/decisions.md) § 0009).
- **The matrix is arm by arm**: an arm is a TREATMENT over its replicate campaigns, each bench row's replicate means differenced and the row the cluster (`scoring/paired.py::read_arms`); Holm gates on the lift's `TestFamily`, BH's q rides the contrast; a pick's election column never enters this matrix.
- **A row carries who authored the point, `cached_samples` and `human_intervened`**: a babysat cycle pairs with nothing.
- **`scored_cells` is the intersection, `covered_cells` the union; every pair is keyed on `Sample.key`**, Holm-corrected across pairs, never across metrics.
- **A ranked edit is a searchpoint against its own campaign's origin, keyed on `sp_hash`; never pool across campaigns.**
- **The environment table reads the roster on the variance split's cells**: `snr` over the pooled residual, Friedman on cells every subject scored, a held-out cell never pooled with search rows. Noise is `EditSpread` over a replicate ARM.
