# application/evidence/ — what a set of subjects jointly says

**Read-only**: the read names a leader, and graduating one is a hand-edit. None of these rules is
readable off the code.

- **A subject is what one channel is anchored on** — a `campaign` (its root origin), a `course` (one branch, at the selection its run stands on — `RunStanding.selection`, joined on label) or a `candidate` (one searchpoint). All three resolve to ONE searchpoint's rows and reduce through `cell_channels`, so pairing, decomposition, power and confound are one arithmetic that knows nothing about which kind produced it. `parse_subject` owns the grammar for every entry point.
- **A candidate's cells are the ARCHIVE's rows, at every address its cycle's ledger names for that INDIVIDUAL** — its walk as an arm, each re-score as a parent, the overlap top-ups and its verify passes, one cell per sample (`read.py::_Cycle`), graded here under the campaign's scorer — never a round file's copy and never a ledger record's, which is one formula's grade over a narrowed payload. So a round closed, in flight or dead mid-walk is ONE read with every channel on it. `SubjectReading.status` is `minted` while the walk is open: every figure on that row is a PREFIX of `expected_samples`, and pairing it is pairing on the cells it happens to have reached. A round the ledger entered again is displaced and answers nothing; an arm its round CARRIED has no id to be addressed by until its score report lands.
- **No L4 gate, no dataset scope, no depth limit.** An inner run is addressed by naming the sandbox chain it lives in, and a sandbox is structurally an ordinary projects tree, so every resolver works unchanged at any depth. Leaf ids alone name nothing — an inner `cycle_id` repeats across sibling sandboxes — so `SubjectReading.inside` completes the address.
- **The origin half answers at round 0 and auto-loads; the three opt-in reads do not.** `ranking=`, `winner_chain=` and `config=` each cost payload or a walk. Config is RESOLVED, never the sparse override: two searchpoints from different campaigns share no delta to line up on.
- **The head-to-head auto-loads too, and reads the BENCH, never the search rows** each optimizer chose among. **Every arm is read off its campaign's result under ONE grader** — the passes and the line's cost, whichever cycle a rebase ended it on; the grader is the ONE scorer every campaign read declares, and a selection declaring two is refused rather than read under either (`comparison_grader`), served as `Evidence.scorer_id` — never off a headline its own run cached. **The comparability guard LABELS a pair and refuses none** (`head_to_head.py::pair_guard`, the ONE deriver, served as `PairGuard` on every pair, every row and the table): two headlines are one quantity on ONE bench set, budget and optimizer model set, neither run steered — every `BenchSet` field equal (`domain/campaign.py::bench_set_of`, the instrument and the origin included), every optimizer llm node on the same models, and a shared origin read alike on its rows — under one formula, a gap there is a backend or judge that moved, which no stamp names. Its state is `controlled` (one quantity, every campaign an arm of the one declaration), `uncontrolled` (one quantity, undeclared) or `differs`, with what it differs on. Every two graded campaigns of distinct treatments are paired through `read_pair`, which alone may refuse one; a pair whose guard `differs` is read and sits OUTSIDE the Holm family. Two arms of one treatment contrast nothing and never pair. A row's guard is its own fact, so one foreign arm demotes no other. An arm of that record still ungraded withholds the verdict (`verdict_absent`). **Cost ratios price INCURRED USD, never the bill**: concurrent arms split a shared cell's bill by who reached it first. **The holdout's reuse is served, never hidden** (`row.bench.status.reads_before`) — owned by [`../../../docs/research/benchmarks.md`](../../../docs/research/benchmarks.md) § The winner's own number.
- **A MASK rides the ADDRESS** (`;lens=`, `;samples=`), so the record and the counterfactual pool as two channels of one read. The lens ENDS where the two readings part (`mask/scenario.py::scenario_spine`) — the same round a fork applying it is minted at — and `ScenarioReading.note` serves why nothing past it is claimed. Boundary owned by `docs/operations/mask-projection.md`.
- **Everything is in the SELECTED metric's units, and `fitness` is ONE formula's** — the one the head-to-head reads its bench under, so no column pools numbers produced by different formulas.
- **The measurand is evaluated PER CELL and only then collapsed.** A campaign-level scalar cannot cancel cell difficulty, so collapsing first would cost the paired test. `cell_channels` is the ONE row-to-number path, and `expr:` deliberately reaches WIDER than the served namespace — naming a hidden channel must reach *unscorable*, never a refusal.
- **Every channel is a fact about the CELL**, and on the recursion a cell IS a whole inner campaign, so its lift, origin, cost and round count are that seed's own (`InnerCellFacts`). A channel a row cannot answer is ABSENT; one it answers with **0.0 is a MEASUREMENT**, so `x or y` between two sources reports a free cell as an unpriced one.
- **A row says WHO authored the point, or the read computes a contrast it cannot LABEL.** A human editing a prompt and L1 editing one are the same act — a configuration proposed against evidence — so they are one measured object needing two keys, and `arm_id` is not the second. `subjects.py::authorship_of` is that key, folded off `IndividualLineage.source` plus the fork's `issued_by`. `cached_samples` is the EFFORT half beside it: a rewind fork inherits its parent's rows, so two subjects matched on cell count are not matched on evidence bought. `human_intervened` is neither — it is about the RUN, and a babysat cycle pairs with nothing however it was authored.
- **A row says which facts are the POINT's and which the CYCLE's.** `round` is the searchpoint's own; `cycle_spend_usd` / `cycle_rounds_scored` are the whole branch's — read either as the point's and a candidate at round 2 of six reports six. No round carries a spend, so cost-to-a-POINT stays unservable.
- **Two cell axes answer different questions**: `scored_cells` is the intersection the variance split is over, `covered_cells` the union the charts plot; a PAIR reads the cells its own two subjects scored, which neither axis is. A blank cell is either unmeasured or `unscorable_cells` — NAMED rather than counted, so a surface renders them as the different facts they are. A subject that answered nothing rides `unread_subjects` rather than thinning the roster silently. **Every pair is a `PairedReading`** (`comparison.py::pair_subjects`, the one `read_pair` call): every two scored subjects are served, one not read carrying the state that says why, keyed on `Sample.key` so two campaigns numbering a cell apart still pair. The lift is Student-t beside the `p_floor` an exact sign test could reach, Holm-corrected across PAIRS and **never across metrics**, which the surface has to say out loud. `hit` is the same pair on the hit grade, whose flips and `gained` / `lost` ids are what a level nets against each other.
- **A ranked edit is a SEARCHPOINT against its own campaign's origin, keyed on `sp_hash`, pooled within ONE campaign.** The identity is the archive join already stamped on every candidate row, so a prompt-only edit ranks like a node-config one — never key on the sparse `pipeline_overlay`, empty for exactly the edit the table exists for. Its `reading` is that pair on every cell both scored in the campaign; the read edits rank by lift under one Holm family and an unread one follows them, unranked. The anchor is excluded by EQUALITY with round 0's own hash, which is why no round is skipped and why an unstamped row groups with nothing rather than under `""`. **Never pool across campaigns:** a delta is relative to a parent and two campaigns share none (`_config_of`), so a pooled number lines up on nothing.
- **No noise term beside `EditSpread`:** the inner instrument is content-addressed, so a second reading of one (state, cell) replays and its spread is zero by construction. A replicate ARM is the honest noise reading; depth on one candidate is `verify`'s job.
