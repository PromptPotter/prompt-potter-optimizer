# application/evidence/ — what a set of subjects jointly says

**Read-only**: the read names a leader, and graduating one is a hand-edit. None of these rules is
readable off the code.

- **A subject is what one channel is anchored on** — a `campaign` (its root origin), a `course` (one branch, at the winner its last ELECTION crowned, joined on label) or a `candidate` (one searchpoint). All three resolve to ONE searchpoint's rows and reduce through `cell_channels`, so pairing, decomposition, power and confound are one arithmetic that knows nothing about which kind produced it. `parse_subject` owns the grammar for every entry point.
- **No L4 gate, no dataset scope, no depth limit.** An inner run is addressed by naming the sandbox chain it lives in, and a sandbox is structurally an ordinary projects tree, so every resolver works unchanged at any depth. Leaf ids alone name nothing — an inner `cycle_id` repeats across sibling sandboxes — so `SubjectReading.inside` completes the address.
- **The origin half answers at round 0 and auto-loads; the three opt-in reads do not.** `ranking=`, `winner_chain=` and `config=` each cost payload or a walk. Config is RESOLVED, never the sparse override: two searchpoints from different campaigns share no delta to line up on.
- **A MASK rides the ADDRESS** (`;lens=`, `;samples=`), so the record and the counterfactual pool as two channels of one read. The lens ENDS where the two readings part (`mask/scenario.py::scenario_spine`) — the same round a fork applying it is minted at — and `ScenarioReading.note` serves why nothing past it is claimed. Boundary owned by `docs/operations/mask-projection.md`.
- **Everything is in the SELECTED metric's units**, never composite fitness, which pools numbers produced by different formulas.
- **The measurand is evaluated PER CELL and only then collapsed.** A campaign-level scalar cannot cancel cell difficulty, so collapsing first would cost the paired test. `cell_channels` is the ONE row-to-number path, and `expr:` deliberately reaches WIDER than the served namespace — naming a hidden channel must reach *unscorable*, never a refusal.
- **Every channel is a fact about the CELL**, and on the recursion a cell IS a whole inner campaign, so its lift, origin, cost and round count are that seed's own (`InnerCellFacts`). A channel a row cannot answer is ABSENT; one it answers with **0.0 is a MEASUREMENT**, so `x or y` between two sources reports a free cell as an unpriced one.
- **A row says WHO authored the point, or the read computes a contrast it cannot LABEL.** A human editing a prompt and L1 editing one are the same act — a configuration proposed against evidence — so they are one measured object needing two keys, and `arm_id` is not the second: it hashes round 0's optimizer prompts, which two forks of one campaign share whoever wrote the edit. `authorship_of` is that key, folded off `IndividualLineage.source` plus the fork's `issued_by` and joined on `(round, label)` because a resume re-mints every id. `cached_samples` is the EFFORT half beside it: a rewind fork inherits its parent's rows, so two subjects matched on cell count are not matched on evidence bought. `human_intervened` is neither — it is about the RUN, and a babysat cycle pairs with nothing however it was authored.
- **A row says which facts are the POINT's and which the CYCLE's.** `round` is the searchpoint's own; `cycle_spend_usd` / `cycle_rounds_scored` are the whole branch's — read either as the point's and a candidate at round 2 of six reports six. No round document carries a spend, so cost-to-a-POINT stays unservable.
- **Two cell axes answer different questions**: `scored_cells` is the intersection the pairing and variance split are over, `covered_cells` the union the charts plot. A blank cell is either unmeasured or `unscorable_cells` — NAMED rather than counted, so a surface renders them as the different facts they are. A subject that answered nothing rides `unread_subjects` rather than thinning the roster silently. Pairwise is exact on the cells both scored, Holm-corrected across PAIRS and **never across metrics**, which the surface has to say out loud.
- **A ranked edit is a SEARCHPOINT against its own campaign's origin, keyed on `sp_hash`, pooled within ONE campaign.** The identity is the archive join already stamped on every candidate row, so a prompt-only edit ranks like a node-config one — keyed on the sparse `pipeline_overlay` it could not, and an overlay is empty for exactly the edit the table exists for. The anchor is excluded by EQUALITY with round 0's own hash, which is why no round is skipped and why an unstamped row groups with nothing rather than under `""`. **Cross-campaign pooling is withdrawn, not lost:** a delta is relative to a parent and two campaigns share none (`_config_of`), so the pooled number was lined up on nothing — do not re-add it as an improvement.
- **No noise term beside `EditSpread`:** the inner instrument is content-addressed, so a second reading of one (state, cell) replays and its spread is zero by construction. A replicate ARM is the honest noise reading; depth on one candidate is `verify`'s job.
