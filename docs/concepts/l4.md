# L4 — self-optimization

PromptPotter optimizing **its own optimizer prompts**. The outer cycle is a normal cycle; each outer
*sample* runs a whole inner campaign on a pinned benchmark seed, through the connector
`application/runner/inner/spawn.py` on `research/datasets/promptpotter-self/`. CLI/headless,
checkout only: a wheel ships only what a user may start, so it refuses the name.

**This file says what is TRUE. How to run and read one is the `potter-self` skill's**
(`.claude/skills/potter-self/`); every knob value is `dataset.yaml`'s.

## The measurand

`held_out_lift` — the MEAN over the inner rounds of the headline fitness of the prompt the search had
**adopted** by that round on the cell's **held-out** rows, minus the origin's on the same rows
(`domain/l4/proxies.py::bench_level_series`). The held-out rows are drawn beside the origin bank under
the cell's seed, reached by no inner node; `campaign.scoring.per_sample` re-anchors it `x + 0.5`, so
the paired estimator's effect IS the mean held-out lift. It is the area under the inner curve: L4
exists to make inner runs gain sooner, and a round-1 gain counts in every later round.
`mean_round_delta`, the same trajectory on the search split in logits, is a recorded fact and scores
nothing.

- **Held out, not in-sample**: the inner optimizer promotes on a point estimate over a small panel, so
  within-cell selection bias is inner behaviour and may not score.
- **Adopted, not proposed**: a round's value is what it crowns; averaging proposals reads negative for an
  exploring generator.
- **Mean, not endpoint**: the quieter instrument at identical spend.
- **The denominator is the line's round BUDGET**, holding the last adopted level forward across rounds a
  cell never ran; a cell with a round whose selection read nothing is EXCLUDED, never held forward.
- **θ is paired on one ruler**: each cell reads the tenant ruler at the version its outer round opened
  on, less its own parent line, and one fit reads θ_C0 and every later level.
- **ONE term scores**: only the family's `per_sample`. A term that cannot move with the candidate gets
  no vote; no term divides by dollar cost (both caches are content-addressed, so a cost denominator
  measures how often the candidate ran before); no peak reading and no declared target.
- **Time is reported, not scored**: an inner run's seconds and dollars are columns beside the score.
- **An optimizer-owned failure floors, never excludes**: an inner run its optimizer aborted, or whose
  every proposal was spoiled before a cell, is `floored` and scores 0, its lift unmeasured
  (`domain/l4/proxies.py::floor_reason`): the round reads the mean lift over measured cells beside
  the floored share.

## What a panel may claim

**An interval that excludes zero is the evidence; the ordering alone is not.**

- **Served at zero spend** by `application/evidence/` (`python -m promptpotter evidence promptpotter-self
  --ranking`): each arm's paired effect with its interval, comparability, replicates, the variance
  decomposition and run-order confounding. Estimation noise and between-cell spread are two bars,
  never their ratio.
- **No within-cell noise term**: the inner instrument is content-addressed, so asking twice replays.
  Depth on a candidate is `verify`'s job.
- **A cell that failed is not a cell that scored zero**: an errored cell carries no verdict and is
  left out of the evidence (`domain/scoring.py::GradedCell.scored`); a published interval may not read it as 0.0.
- **Absolute outer numbers never travel across runs**; within a run, seeds pair under CRN.
- **A HIT/MISS panel stays silent at L4**: the cell is verifier-graded, so no cell can be a hit.

## Invariants — break one and the corpus is void, silently

- **The inner scorer, formula, seeds and held-out cells are unreachable by the outer loop**, and none can
  be a `pipeline_param` ([`../research/external-constraints.md`](../research/external-constraints.md) § Ranked, item 1).
- **`application/runner/inner/spawn.py::_identity_config` enumerates the inner-origin fingerprint**; a
  dispatch renderer or an estimator moves it exactly as an inner prompt body does, and a seat's pin
  edit re-keys that cell (its key, depth inside, is `domain/l4/cells.py::InnerTaskSpec`'s). The panel
  resolves at each of its three reads (graph, identity, the run's cells) and the inner session loads
  live, so an edit made mid-run lands at the next spawn and silently re-partitions the corpus.
- **The solver's SPEND AXES are the instrument; the inner search writes prompt text alone**: model and
  provider are the panel's ENVIRONMENT axis; `reasoning_effort`, `max_tokens` and `temperature` are rails.
- **The held-out rows are identical across arms**; a dataset whose rows beyond the bank leave fewer than
  `n_samples_bench` is refused before any send. Every inner cell carries `dataset_split: {bench: 0}`
  (`application/runner/inner/tasks.py`).
- **The instrument is decided per CELL** (`domain/cells.py::Seat`); two cells running one node under
  different overlays refuse. A cell runs the optimizer its own dataset names and is keyed by its own
  instrument alone.
- **An inner θ never crosses a dataset; a lift pairs on shared cells.**
- **`max_inner_rounds ≥ 2`, and an inner run carries no lives**: the round budget is the width of
  the area a cell is scored on, and a cell its optimizer stops sooner holds its last level across it.
  **Cells per round > `elimination_n_min`, and ≥ 6** — below that θ_se exceeds the point-lift.
- **CRN is the variance control, and the only one**; an identical cell replays.
- **`budget.tokens` stays unbounded** — inner tokens land on the outer ledger; `budget.usd` is the cap.

## Depth

L4 runs one level deep (`measurement_context.py::MAX_INSTRUMENT_DEPTH` is 1): a depth-1 inner cycle
refuses to spawn, since nothing uses a nested L4 and each level widens what a sandbox may hold. To
bring depth 2 back, raise the cap and make the sandbox reclaim exact first: the owner record
(`store/layout.py::SandboxOwner`) carries the owner's whole `inside` chain, and the reaper resolves the
owner's tree off it and keeps the sandbox while the owner's producer lock is held, never deciding
on a load that failed in the wrong tree.

## Cost

Geometric: one outer round is `(1 origin + n_variants) × n_inner_tasks` fresh inner campaigns. **`budget.usd`
is a cap, not an estimate**, and one too small to finish a round buys nothing. `python -m promptpotter
evidence promptpotter-self` answers variance and power on demand.

## Refuted

Four optimizer-prompt edits are refuted — do not re-propose them: slot-steering language, an
anti-same-slot clause, a reweight of under-cited panels, and a wire `maxLength` on
`prompt_fields_updates`. A prompt clause telling the model to ignore a silent panel is not a fix either.
