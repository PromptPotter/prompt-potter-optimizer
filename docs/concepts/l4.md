# L4 — self-optimization

PromptPotter optimizing **its own optimizer prompts**. The outer cycle is a normal cycle; each outer
*sample* runs a whole inner campaign on a pinned benchmark seed, through the connector
`application/runner/inner/spawn.py` on `research/datasets/promptpotter-self/`. CLI/headless,
checkout only: a wheel ships only what a user may start, so it refuses the name.

**This file says what is TRUE. How to run and read one is the `potter-self` skill's**
(`.claude/skills/potter-self/`); every knob value is `family.yaml`'s or a member's (`dev`, `run`).

## The measurand

`held_out_lift` — the area of the inner line's held-out curve (`application/bench/line.py::read_curve`,
carried as `domain/bench.py::BenchCurve`): per held-out row, the MEAN over the round budget of the
headline grade of the prompt the search had **adopted** by that round, minus the origin's on that row,
read by one paired t over the rows every graded pass scored. Every adoption is graded at the line's
end (`bench_trigger: every_adoption`). The held-out rows are the laid `dataset_split.bench`, ranked
by the cell's seed and reached by no inner node; `campaign.scoring.per_sample` re-anchors it
`x + 0.5`, so the paired estimator's effect IS the mean held-out lift. It is the area under the inner curve: L4
exists to make inner runs gain sooner, and a round-1 gain counts in every later round.
`held_out_lift_se`, that t's SE, rides beside it as a reported key no formula reads, null where the
estimate took none; `domain/l4/proxies.py::panel_precision` separates cells on the two.
`mean_round_delta`, the same trajectory on the search split in logits, is a column of the cell's
`inner` facts, never a term: nothing ranks on it.

- **Held out, not in-sample**: the inner optimizer promotes on a point estimate over a small panel, so
  within-cell selection bias is inner behaviour and may not score.
- **Adopted, not proposed**: a round's value is what it crowns; averaging proposals reads negative for an
  exploring generator.
- **Mean, not endpoint**: the quieter instrument at identical spend.
- **The denominator is the line's round BUDGET**, holding the last adopted level forward across rounds a
  cell never ran; a cell whose area does not read (a graded pass read nothing) is EXCLUDED, never held
  forward, and a line that never adopted reads 0.0.
- **θ is paired on one ruler**: each cell reads the tenant ruler at the version its outer round opened
  on, less its own parent line, and one fit reads θ_C0 and every later level.
- **ONE term scores**: only the family's `per_sample`. A term that cannot move with the candidate gets
  no vote; no term divides by dollar cost (both caches are content-addressed, so a cost denominator
  measures how often the candidate ran before); no peak reading and no declared target.
- **One inner grade and one stall ladder across seats**: the panel's `inner_campaign` declares the
  inner `scoring` once, its `inner_nodes` each optimizer's nodes (its ladder among them), and no
  seat's own reaches its inner run (`runner/inner/tasks.py::InnerTasks._laid`), so any optimizer
  may sit a seat and a lift on one benchmark reads on another's scale;
  `runner/inner/tasks.py::_lever` names both, and a panel whose cells diverge refuses.
- **Time is reported, not scored**: an inner run's seconds and dollars are columns beside the score.
- **An optimizer-owned failure floors, never excludes**: an inner run its optimizer aborted, or whose
  every proposal over the rounds its line spent (a rebase's discarded ones included) was spoiled
  before a cell, is `floored` and scores 0, its lift unmeasured
  (`domain/l4/proxies.py::floor_reason`): the round reads the mean lift over measured cells beside
  the floored share. An abort is the optimizer's only where its line filed no cell the configuration
  does not answer for (`domain/l4/proxies.py::abort_owner`); an upstream-owned one is measured over
  the rounds it ran.

## What a panel may claim

**An interval that excludes zero is the evidence; the ordering alone is not.**

- **An outer crown is verified on held-out seats, as any run's selection is**: a seat declared
  `held_out` is a bench row of the outer campaign (the family's `dataset_split`), never drawn by the
  outer search; the origin and each outer adoption are graded on it once (`bench_trigger:
  every_adoption`), and `held-out` serves the reading as any run's bench. The seat stays out of the
  cell's key (`domain/cells.py::Seat.pin`), so a held-out seat's cells replay once it is searched.

- **Served at zero spend** by `application/evidence/` (`python -m promptpotter evidence promptpotter-self
  --ranking`): each arm's paired effect with its interval, comparability, replicates, the variance
  decomposition and run-order confounding. Estimation noise and between-cell spread are two bars,
  never their ratio.
- **No within-cell noise term**: the inner instrument is content-addressed, so asking twice replays.
  Depth on a candidate is `verify`'s job.
- **A cell that failed is not a cell that scored zero**: an errored cell carries no verdict and is
  left out of the evidence (`domain/scoring.py::GradedCell.scored`); a published interval may not read it as 0.0.
- **Absolute outer numbers never travel across runs**; within a run, seeds pair under CRN.
- **A HIT/MISS panel stays silent at L4**: the cell is verifier-graded, so no cell can be a hit.

## Invariants — break one and the corpus is void, silently

- **The inner scorer, formula, seeds and held-out cells are unreachable by the outer loop**, and none can
  be a `pipeline_param` ([`../research/external-constraints.md`](../research/external-constraints.md) § Ranked, item 1).
- **`application/runner/inner/spawn.py::_identity_config` enumerates the inner-origin fingerprint**; a
  dispatch renderer or an estimator moves it exactly as an inner prompt body does, and a seat's pin
  edit re-keys that cell. Its key is `domain/l4/cells.py::cell_key`: the root cycle id the seat's
  laid session mints (its origin hash and measured address, so an inner `task.md`, `origin.yaml` or
  `source:` edit moves it), its depth and treatment, read by `optimizer_contract.py::BehaviourKeys.cell_estimator`
  (the L4 leg's inner campaigns' behaviour, never its outer's requests). The keys are laid once, at
  the outer run's init (`runner/inner/spawn.py::panel_rows`, the panel's one bank build), while the
  inner session loads live, so an edit made mid-run lands at the next spawn under the init's key.
- **The inner optimizer prompts are the outer target's prompt fields**: each inner llm node is served
  field-wise with its template as its start (`domain/l4/cells.py::InnerCells.pipeline`), so the outer
  origin is that start, and a cell runs only the fields an arm moved off it (`InnerCells.own`).
- **The solver's SPEND AXES are the instrument; the inner search writes prompt text alone**: model and
  provider are the panel's ENVIRONMENT axis; `reasoning_effort`, `max_tokens` and `temperature` are rails.
- **The held-out rows are identical across arms**: a seat's geometry is the panel's `inner_campaign`,
  a `campaign:` partial laid over the seat dataset's own block (`application/runner/inner/tasks.py::InnerTasks`),
  refused at resolve unless it lays `budget.rounds ≥ 2`, a `dataset_split.bench` and
  `bench_trigger: every_adoption`.
- **The instrument is decided per CELL** (`domain/cells.py::Seat`); two cells running one node under
  different overlays refuse. A cell runs the optimizer its own dataset names and is keyed by its own
  instrument alone.
- **An inner θ never crosses a dataset; a lift pairs on shared cells.**
- **`inner_campaign.optimization.budget.rounds ≥ 2`, and an inner run carries no lives**: the round budget is the width of
  the area a cell is scored on, and a cell its optimizer stops sooner holds its last level across it.
  **Cells per round > `elimination_n_min`, and ≥ 6** — below that θ_se exceeds the point-lift.
- **CRN is the variance control, and the only one**; an identical cell replays.
- **`budget.tokens` stays unbounded** — inner tokens land on the outer ledger; `budget.usd` is the cap.

## Depth

L4 runs one level deep, by ONE door: resolve refuses a panel seat on a self-optimization dataset
(`runner/inner/tasks.py::InnerTasks.resolve_cells`, `pipeline_config_invalid`), so no inner run's
connector spawns, since nothing uses a nested L4 and each level widens what a sandbox may hold. A
spawn checks no depth: only a self-optimization connector reaches `spawn.py::run_inner_cycle`, and
resolve seats none inside one. To bring depth 2 back, lift that refusal and make the sandbox reclaim
exact first: the owner record
(`store/layout.py::SandboxOwner`) carries the owner's whole `inside` chain, and the reaper resolves the
owner's tree off it and keeps the sandbox while the owner's producer lock is held, never deciding
on a load that failed in the wrong tree.

## Cost

Geometric: one outer round is `(1 origin + n_variants) × n_inner_tasks` fresh inner campaigns, and
each held-out seat adds one for the origin and one per outer adoption. **`budget.usd`
is a cap, not an estimate**, and one too small to finish a round buys nothing. `python -m promptpotter
evidence promptpotter-self` answers variance and power on demand.

## Refuted

Four optimizer-prompt edits are refuted — do not re-propose them: slot-steering language, an
anti-same-slot clause, a reweight of under-cited panels, and a wire `maxLength` on the
prompt-field text L1 writes. A prompt clause telling the model to ignore a silent panel is not a fix either.
