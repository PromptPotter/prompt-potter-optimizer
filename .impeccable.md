## Design Context

### Users
Non-technical consumers, entrepreneurs, and small business owners evaluating AI-assisted design tools. They arrive curious but skeptical — they need to quickly understand what Impeccable does and trust that it's the professional choice. They are not developers or designers; the site must communicate value without jargon.

### Brand Personality
**Refined, authoritative, approachable.** The site should feel like a well-edited magazine — not a SaaS landing page and not a design agency portfolio. It earns trust through restraint and craft, not through noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Calm confidence. High-contrast editorial with generous whitespace. Light mode only.
- **Typography:** Noto Sans as the primary typeface. Use weight and size for hierarchy, not font variety.
- **Color:** Near-white paper, near-black ink, vibrant magenta/rose accent (`oklch(60% 0.25 350)`) used sparingly for emphasis. Warm cream background tones.
- **Texture:** Subtle grain overlay for depth. Glassmorphism on navigation (backdrop blur, translucent backgrounds).
- **Motion:** Purposeful and subtle — reveal animations, smooth transitions. Never gratuitous.
- **Anti-references:** Must NOT look like generic AI/SaaS (purple gradients, Inter font, rounded cards-on-cards) — these are the exact anti-patterns Impeccable critiques. Also must NOT look like over-designed agency work (parallax overload, animation for its own sake).

### Accessibility
Target WCAG AAA compliance: enhanced contrast ratios (7:1 for normal text, 4.5:1 for large text), comprehensive keyboard navigation, reduced motion support (`prefers-reduced-motion`), thorough alt text and ARIA labeling, clear focus indicators.

### Design Principles
1. **Practice what you preach** — The site is the product's proof. Every design choice must exemplify the quality Impeccable promises. If we critique purple gradients, we cannot use them.
2. **Clarity over cleverness** — The audience is non-technical. Every element must communicate instantly. Hierarchy guides the eye; nothing competes for attention.
3. **Restraint is confidence** — Whitespace, limited color palette, and typographic discipline signal mastery. Resist the urge to add — subtract instead.
4. **Accessible by default** — WCAG AAA is not a checkbox; it's a design constraint that makes everything better. High contrast, clear focus states, motion respect.
5. **Substance over spectacle** — Animations and interactions must serve comprehension, not decoration. Every motion earns its frame budget.
