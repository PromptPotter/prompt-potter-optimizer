# VOICE.md — Copy register & language

> Single source of truth for **tone, copy register, and language** across this repo, the webapp, and the sibling marketing repo `promptpotter-web`. Visual identity (palette, themes, mark, motion) lives in [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md).

The value proposition we say plainly: **"fix a broken LLM pipeline in one hour of waiting, then it just works."**

## Two registers, one brand

Copy register swaps with the theme (theme = audience selector — see [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md)). Same wordmark, same Potter portrait; different words, density, and motion budget.

- **Light (central register): refined, demystifying, research-grade.** Editorial calm at moderate density. Plain language with a thin craft register — the Potter and the wordmark carry the charm; the copy does not. The Potter is a hint, not a costume. The buyer's register, and the operator's by default.
- **Dark (alternate operator mode): refined, authoritative, raw power.** Editorial calm at high information density. Terminal-adjacent. DOOM / lava / galactic energy without theatrics. The brand reads as **Superman-tier force multiplier**, not a friendly wizard.

## Language rules

- **No craft-mysticism, ever.** No "brew / spell / potion / magic helper" anywhere — that framing was explicitly retired. This holds in both registers.
- **Plain language carries meaning; charm lives in the mark.** Copy demystifies; the Potter portrait and wordmark do the personality work.
- **Name the mascot correctly.** Always "the Potter" — never bare "wizard," never bare "Potter."
- **`PromptPotter` is literal text.** Always rendered as text in branded imagery, never implied or stylized away (see [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md) for the visual side).
- **Domain framing.** PromptPotter = LLM-driven program evolution; new names and sentences draw on: evolve, generation, population, fitness, mutation, selection, individual.
- **Spend, not patience.** Operator-facing surfaces expose spend ($/tokens) as the headline cost; patience/FSM bookkeeping stays internal.

## Anti-references — what the copy must NOT sound like

- **Friendly-wizard helper** — no whimsical mascot voice, no craft-laden copy ("brew", "potion", "spell", "magic helper"), no cartoon charm. The Potter is a force multiplier, not a pet.
- **Generic AI/SaaS pitch** — no breathless "supercharge your workflow" filler, no ChatGPT-wrapper hero copy.

## Where copy lives

Marketing copy, hero lines, CTAs, and the waitlist form live in the sibling repo `promptpotter-web`. This document is the register both repos write to; the visual tokens they share are specified in [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md).
